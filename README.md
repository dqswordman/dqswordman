## Research and open source

### Complex Utility, Simple Calibration

**WSDM 2027 Findings** · Decision sufficiency in RAG evidence selection

A study of how far a small number of utility measurements can take evidence selection in multi-hop question answering. The artifact includes the decision policies, archived scores, and CPU replay.
